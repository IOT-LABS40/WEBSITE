Privacy and cookie policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This page explains which personal data is processed when you visit iotlabs.cc or write to us, what it is used for, who it is shared with and how you can exercise your rights. It also lists the cookies used on the site.

Controller Data and purposes Providers Cookies Your rights Changes

01Data controller

IOTLABS is a personal project; there is currently no incorporated company behind it. The controller of your data is the individual who owns the project.

For any privacy question, write to info@iotlabs.cc.

03Providers and international transfers

To run the site we use the following providers, which may process some of your data on our behalf:

  • Cloudflare, Inc.: website hosting and content delivery network.
  • Google LLC: Google Analytics (only with your consent) and Google Fonts. The site’s fonts are downloaded from Google’s servers, which receive your IP address.
  • Microsoft Corporation: Microsoft Clarity (only with your consent).
  • The email provider we use for the info@iotlabs.cc account.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Cloudflare, Google and Microsoft participate in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which provides adequate safeguards for these transfers.

How to manage cookies

You can accept, reject or change your choice at any time with the button below or the “Cookie settings” link in the footer. If you withdraw your consent, we delete the analytics cookies from our domain. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.

05Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability, and withdraw your consent, at any time by writing to info@iotlabs.cc. We will reply within one month at the latest.

If you believe we have not handled your data properly, you can lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country.

06Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the site, the tools we use or the law change. The current version and its update date will always be published here.
